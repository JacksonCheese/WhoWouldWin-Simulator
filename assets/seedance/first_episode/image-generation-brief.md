# First episode image-generation brief

These 26 selected PNGs are **development reference art** for eight manually generated Seedance shots. They were generated with the built-in still-image tool; no Seedance or paid video provider was called. The two supplied example videos were studied for simplified silhouette, flat color, sparse background, short cuts, and legible impact. Frames from those videos were not placed in this asset pack.

## Visual lock

- Full-frame vertical 9:16 keyframes; separate square character and ability boards.
- Bold dark outlines, simple heads and facial marks, orange/charcoal Naruto versus broad red/off-white Omni-Man.
- Stable blue-hour street with one road stripe and two blue-gray facade masses.
- One readable action per frame, visible limb separation, local blue energy effects, no photorealism or 3D rendering.
- Human review is still needed: the generated art has more background detail than the simplest reference and can crop extremities near frame edges. Do not treat it as final animation approval.

## Source truth and ability language

The saved seed-69 fight includes `charged_vortex` and `energy_orb` projectile events. The first is visually treated as a **Rasenshuriken-like thrown wind shuriken** without changing its simulator ability ID. The second is a **generic Energy Orb** placeholder projectile, not Rasengan and not Rasenshuriken. The optional `rasengan-simple.png` board depicts a hand-held Rasengan and is deliberately not staged into either projectile shot. A genuine Rasengan hit must be hand-delivered and would require a recorded melee/contact event or a clearly separate noncanonical concept sequence.

## Art selection

- Ten character boards (front, side, three-quarter, action, and silhouette for each fighter) lock proportions and costume colors.
- `simplified-style-reference.png` and `vertical-background-reference.png` lock art treatment and location.
- `naruto-chakra-form-simple.png`, `omniman-grapple-simple.png`, `omniman-impact-simple.png`, `charged-vortex-projectile.png`, and `energy-orb-projectile.png` constrain scene-specific visual language. `rasengan-simple.png` is retained only for future hand-contact work.
- Eight keyframes show faceoff, charged-vortex hit, grapple dodge, guarded heavy strike, transformation, Omni-Man charge hit, Energy Orb KO, and aftermath in canonical order.

Original generated files remain in Codex's generated-image directory. The selected PNGs here are the reproducible source assets copied into the ignored local upload package. `generated_image_inventory.json` in that package records their hashes and pending human approval.
