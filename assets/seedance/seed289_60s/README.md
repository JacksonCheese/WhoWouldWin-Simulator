# Seed-289 image sources

This directory is the reproducible source for the 62-second, 28-shot manual Seedance package. `source_replay.json` is the unmodified authoritative seed-289 replay. `keyframes/shot_001.png` through `shot_028.png` are 9:16 development boards generated for the directed edit and visually reviewed as a contact sheet. `energy-orb-straight.png` is a separate reference for the recorded generic ranged Energy Orb.

Generate the upload package with `python -m whowouldwin.cli.main prepare-seedance-longform`. The generated `outputs/` folder stays out of Git and can be rebuilt from these assets. Existing character, style, chakra, grapple, heavy-strike and Rasengan reference boards are sourced from `assets/seedance/first_episode/shared_references/` and copied during packaging.

The Rasengan boards in shots 22–26 show a **noncanonical, failed, hand-held melee attempt** approved for editorial staging. It is never thrown and causes no hit or damage. Shot 9 shows the separate recorded charged-vortex projectile, drawn as a Rasenshuriken-like wind shuriken. Shot 13 shows the separate recorded generic Energy Orb as a small straight pellet with no swirl. Omni-Man's recorded heavy strike remains the finishing blow.

Camera compositions vary across wide, low, overhead, profile, three-quarter, over-the-shoulder, reaction close-up and oblique near-contact views. The contact sheet in the generated package is the visual index. The images are development inputs for video testing, not proof that final motion or continuity will pass.
